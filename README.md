# Membri.app

Membership with Memory.

Life is a membership. Universal membership that remembers your purchases and visits, rewards repeat business, and lets spending lower next year's fee.

## Landing Page

The `index.html` is the landing page.

## Concept

- One membership that travels with you.
- Link cards and phone pay.
- Merchants remember you and reward repeat visits.
- Select purchases reduce the next annual membership fee.
- AI powers the accounting and attribution.
